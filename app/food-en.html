What can rabbits eat?

The food book of Ren and Jill, two office bunnies

For rabbits, hay comes first, plus a few pellets and greens and the occasional treat. This page collects the food book from our game, Hay Dash.

This is general guidance. The right amounts depend on size, age and health; ask a vet if you are unsure.

Staples (every day)

  • Timothy hay: The staple: grass hay, available at all times. The fiber keeps the gut moving and all that chewing wears the teeth down.
  • Pellets: A complete rabbit food. Guides vary (about 1.5–5% of body weight a day); err on the low side and split it between morning and evening.

Vegetables (a little at a time)

  • Komatsuna: Japanese mustard spinach, a dark leafy green. Leafy greens should be most of the veggies; wash, dry and give a little at a time.
  • Bok choy: A popular leafy green. Introduce any new vegetable in small amounts.
  • Parsley: Strong-smelling and loved by many bunnies. Just a small sprig.
  • Carrot tops: The leafy top has less sugar than the root. Wash well and give a little.
  • Dandelion: A classic wild green. Only from places free of pesticides and pet droppings, in small amounts (Nagano guide). Too much can loosen stools.
  • Four-leaf clover: Clover is a wild green too. A four-leaf one is rare and lucky.

Treats (just a little)

  • Apple: Fruit is a treat. Remove the core and seeds and give a thin slice.
  • Strawberry: Sweet and loved. Just a little, as a reward.
  • Banana: Very sugary. One small piece is plenty.
  • Pineapple: Said to help digestion (Nagano guide). Fresh or dried, a small amount.
  • Carrot: A favorite, but sugary, so it counts as a treat: a little, once or twice a week.
  • Papaya: Said to help digestion (Nagano guide). Dried papaya is a classic treat. Small amounts.
  • Alfalfa: A legume hay, high in protein and calcium, so better for young bunnies. Adults should not have too much (Nagano guide).

Never feed

  • Chocolate: Contains theobromine, which is toxic to rabbits. Never feed it.
  • Onions & leeks: Onions and their relatives (leeks, garlic) are poisonous to rabbits (Nagano guide).
  • Potato chips: Human snacks have far too much salt, fat and sugar (Nagano guide).
  • Sunflower seeds: Very fatty seeds. A hamster favorite, but not for rabbits.
